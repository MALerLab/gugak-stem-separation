⚠️ Same seed ≠ same data. exp004.1 and exp004 draw the SAME n and class set S for every
item index (n and S are drawn before p is consulted — the launch gates reproduce exp004's
n-histogram and per-class exposure to four digits), but from there exp004 spends RNG on
anchors/clusters and exp004.1 does not, so the streams diverge within the item. exp004.1
vs exp002.4 share the incoherent per-stem RNG order but n ~ U{2..9} vs U{1..9} shifts
which item draws what. Holding the seed removes seed as an explanatory variable between
the three arms; it does not make the draw streams comparable item-for-item.
