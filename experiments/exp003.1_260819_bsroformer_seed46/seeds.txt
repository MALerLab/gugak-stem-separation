==================================================================================
All three are 46 — exp003.0 ran 42 on all three. The seed is the ONLY difference between
the two runs (data order, mix draws, head init all move together — total seed spread).

1. HEAD (mask-estimator) INITIALISATION       seed 46
   scripts/init_bsroformer_start_checkpoint.py --seed 46
   Seeds torch's global RNG before the 9-stem model is constructed, which is what draws
   the 9 freshly initialised per-source mask estimators (221,741,640 parameters, 87% of
   the model). Verified reproducible: two independent builds at this seed are
   bit-identical across all 2,595 tensors (--verify-twice). See checkpoint_init_log.txt.

2. MIX DRAW STREAM                            seed 46
   configs/exp003.1_bsroformer_seed46.yaml -> gugak_mix.seed: 46
   Each dataset item draws from np.random.default_rng([seed, index]), so the stream is
   per-item and independent of worker count. Governs: how many classes go in a mix,
   which ones, which source file, which time window, per-stem gain, L/R swap.

3. TRAINER GLOBAL                             seed 46
   experiments/exp003.1_260819_bsroformer_seed46/launch.sh -> --seed 46
   MSST's own initialize_environment seed (python/numpy/torch global RNGs for anything
   the trainer itself does).

Not seeded, and deliberately so: cuDNN/cuBLAS kernel nondeterminism is left on
(deterministic algorithms would cost throughput; matches exp003.0). Two runs of this
config will therefore agree closely but not bit-exactly on the loss curve.
